Quick Reference Card
"Connecting generous donors with students who need a helping hand"
If you want to give
You are a Donor
  • Register once, get your Donor ID
  • Select an item from the approved list & submit
  • Admin will contact you to collect from your address
  • Receive email at every step
  • Track your donation using your Donation ID
If you need support
You are a Recipient
  • Register once, get your Recipient ID
  • Request 1 available item at a time
  • Add your preferred delivery time in the notes
  • Receive email at every step
  • Leave feedback once your item arrives

Good to know
🆔
Save Your ID
Your Donor or Recipient ID is your key. Save it from your welcome email — you need it every time.
📧
Email Updates
You will receive an email at every status change. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for updates.
🔍
Track Anytime
Use the Track tab in your portal with your Donation ID or Request ID to see the current status instantly.
📋
Item Not Listed?
Recipients can submit a Custom Request. Include the item name, size, and why you need it — admin will review.
📝
Leave Feedback
After delivery, leave a quick note in your portal. Your donor will be notified — it means a lot to them!
📱
Works on Mobile
All portals work on your phone. Add the link to your home screen for quick one-tap access.
